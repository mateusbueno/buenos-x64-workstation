Velora Studio: Part Editor, FM and VA Sound

Velora Studio shows every parameter at once, with no menus. For FM and VA the Sound section becomes a grid of group cards; each card is a native control group, so it is fully keyboard operable. Only existing tokens are used (Sálvia Stage and Studio sets, {rounded.md} cards, {rounded.desktop-sm} controls, 2 px selection and focus ring). The window chrome, sidebar and Live Set header are in key-screen-studio.html; this page draws only the title bar, the Part header and the editor.

  • Keyboard. Tab moves through the "Sound groups" jump links, then through each card's controls in reading order; a jump link moves focus to its card (Enter). Arrow keys change sliders, steppers and selects; Space toggles checkboxes; Esc leaves a card back to its jump link. The focus ring is 2 px accent with a 2 px offset (shown on OP 2 / Filter), never colour alone: it is also an outline.
  • BRIGHTNESS. The same fixed encoder as on the device: FM moves the modulator levels of the current algorithm (OP 2 · 4 · 6 for algorithm 5, marked ◐), VA moves the filter cutoff. The read-out sits above the cards.
  • Reflow. 4 columns at 100%, 2 at ≥150%, 1 at ≥200%; the algorithm card spans 2 columns when there are 2 or more. Text uses rem in the product (minimum 13 px body, 11 px labels).
  • Live edit. Changes go to the Velora in real time and are marked with ◆ in the footer until Write to Velora, as for every other Part Editor section.

1 · FM Sound at 100%

Sound is the first of the Part Editor sections and grows to the full width, because FM has dozens of parameters: the routing diagram with algorithm and feedback, six operator cards (ratio, level, envelope), pitch envelope and LFO. The other sections (amp envelope + EQ, effects, zone + arpeggiator + assign) keep their place below.

2 · VA Sound at 100%

Oscillators 1 and 2, mixer, filter, amp and filter envelopes, LFO and voice (mode, unison, glide) as cards in the same grid.

3 · Reflow at 150% and 200% text size

The window stays 900 px wide while the text grows. Sound cards go 4 columns → 2 columns at 150% → 1 column at 200%, and the lower sections 3 → 2 → 1, with no horizontal scrolling; the editor scrolls vertically. (The product sizes in rem; this page uses em so one frame can show each size.)

4 · Not available: Part Strips and Sound section

The Live Set was opened on a Velora whose firmware lacks the engine (rollback, Story 12.8). Strips 2 and 4 keep name and category and show ! NEEDS UPDATE 1.4; the Sound section is replaced by a plain banner with Change timbre and Update firmware. The other sections stay editable. Nothing is substituted and the part is silent on the Velora.
