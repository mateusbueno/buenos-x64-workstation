Velora 73: Launcher Grid

MENU opens the Launcher from any surface (Story 4.15; UX-DR32, UX-DR56, UX-DR102). Seven tiles in two rows, 4 + 3: Setlist Browser, Setlist, Library, Effects, Scenes, Import, Settings. VALUE moves the highlight, WRITE or touch opens the tile (it replaces the Launcher), MENU or EXIT closes to the surface the Launcher was opened from. Sound is never interrupted.

  • Geometry (1024×600). Top Bar 56 · content 488 · Hint Bar 56. Content padding 22 px (screen gutter) sides, 24 px top. Tiles are 233 × 196 px (minimum target is 56 px), gaps 16 px horizontal and vertical (minimum 12 px). Row 2 holds three tiles in columns 1–3; the fourth cell stays empty.
  • Tile. Surface background, 1 px line-strong border, {rounded.md}, glyph in accent, label in {typography.tile-label-large} 20 px, one-line purpose in muted 14 px. Labels wrap to two lines and never truncate.
  • Highlighted tile. Raised background, 3 px accent border ({spacing.selection-device}), lifted 4 px with a shadow: shape and elevation, not colour alone.
  • Disabled tile (surface not yet available). Dashed border instead of solid, transparent fill, muted glyph and label (still ≥ 4.5:1, no opacity on text), a ⊘ NOT AVAILABLE badge and a reason line (“No USB drive found”). It stays reachable by VALUE so the reason can be read; WRITE or touch on it does nothing and the Hint Bar right side repeats the reason when it is highlighted.
  • Hint Bar. VALUE move · WRITE open · MENU close · EXIT close. Control names stay English; verbs localize.
  • Microcopy. Fixed vocabulary (Setlist, Live Set, Scene, Timbre, Library); plain facts, no apology.
