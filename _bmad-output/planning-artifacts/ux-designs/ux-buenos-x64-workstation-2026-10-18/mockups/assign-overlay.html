Velora 73: ASSIGN Overlay

Shown over the Performance part area when an encoder is turned while the PART / ALL / ASSIGN toggle is on ASSIGN. A raised card ({components.assign-overlay}, {rounded.md}) lists K1–K8 in {typography.meta-caps} with the assigned parameter name under each in {typography.tile-label} (16 px, up to two lines, never clipped to one) and the current value. The turned knob's slot (K2 here) is raised with a 3 px accent border and an accent K label, so it is never marked by colour alone. The Top Bar, the Live Set Hero and the Scene strip stay visible and unchanged; only the part area is covered. Drawn at 1024 × 600 (Top Bar 56 · Hero 118 · Scene strip 72 · Part area 298 · Status 56) in the Stage and Studio themes, then with every label lengthened by 40% with accented capitals and user content replaced by ÁÇÊÕÑ Ação.

  • Informational only. The overlay has no controls and takes no focus; sound is never blocked and playing keys sounds as normal. Slots are not touch targets.
  • Timing. It stays for 3 s and restarts on each turn (EXPERIENCE › Timings), then the Part Strips return. Under reduced motion it appears and leaves in place.
  • Precedence. It covers the part area and takes precedence over a Notice Toast, which waits until the overlay closes. It never covers the Hero or the Scene strip.
  • Unassigned. A slot with no mapping reads "Not assigned" in muted text (K8); turning that knob highlights its slot and does nothing else.
  • Per Live Set. The map belongs to the active Live Set; the heading says so. Velora Studio edits it in the Part Editor.
  • Names. Parameter names are system strings and lengthen in translation; they wrap to two lines and then end with an ellipsis. Live Set and scene names are user content and ellipsize at the end.
