4 · Rules & Edge Cases

  • Per-theme drawbar edge rule. Black drawbar heads carry a 2 px edge rule (#667069) in the Stage theme to ensure clear separation against dark slots; white drawbar heads carry a 2 px edge rule (#7d8680) in the Studio theme against light slots.
  • Rotary terminology only. The rotary cabinet simulation is strictly titled ROTARY (or "Rotary Speaker") across all labels and status readouts. Never "Leslie". Speed states are SLOW, FAST, and STOP.
  • Never colour only. Selected segments and active states are marked by accent fill, bold weight, and enclosing borders. Non-selected items remain fully legible with high-contrast text.
  • Numeric registration. Grouped in the standard 9-digit notation 88 8600 000 with tabular numerals so values remain stable without horizontal jitter.
