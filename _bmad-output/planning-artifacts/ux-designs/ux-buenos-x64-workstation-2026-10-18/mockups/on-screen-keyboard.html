Velora 73: On-screen Keyboard (OSK)

The On-screen Keyboard appears over the lower content area when a text field (such as a search or rename input) receives focus. It never covers the Top Bar or the active input field. All keys have a minimum touch target height of 56 px (58 px rendered). The Done key is accent-filled, and accented characters are directly accessible or reachable via hold popovers per the language layout (EN, PT-BR, ES).

  • Key dimensions. 4 rows of keys, each 58 px tall (well exceeding the 56 px touch floor). Total keyboard height = 276 px.
  • Layering rule. Sits at y = 324 to 600 px; Top Bar (0..56 px) and active input field (56..140 px) remain completely visible and un-obscured.
  • Accent coverage rule.
    • PT-BR: Direct Ç key; dead keys ~ and ´ on the numeric/symbol layer, plus press-and-hold vowel popovers for (á, à, â, ã, é, ê, í, ó, ô, õ, ú).
    • ES: Direct Ñ key; dead key ´ and direct hold popovers for (á, é, í, ó, ú, ü, ¿, ¡).
    • EN: Standard QWERTY layout with press-and-hold accents available on all vowels.
  • Physical hardware interaction. Playing keys on the keybed continues sounding normally while the OSK is open. WRITE on the panel confirms (equivalent to Done), and EXIT closes the keyboard keeping the typed text.
