6 · Microcopy & State Rules

  • Rotary wording. The rotary speaker effect is always labeled ROTARY (or "Rotary Speaker"); never "Leslie". Speed states are SLOW, FAST, and STOP.
  • Never colour only. Active vs Bypassed state is communicated via text (ACTIVE vs BYPASS), typography (normal vs struck-through), and border style (solid accent vs dashed border).
  • Hint Bar verbs. The Hint Bar explicitly pairs physical panel controls with clear localized action verbs: VALUE scroll, WRITE select, HOLD ▲▼ reorder, EXIT back.
