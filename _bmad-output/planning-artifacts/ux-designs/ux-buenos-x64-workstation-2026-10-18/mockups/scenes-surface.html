Velora 73: Scenes Surface

The Scenes surface allows the player to inspect, recall, create, rename, duplicate, reorder and clear scenes (slots 1–8) for the current Live Set. Slots are drawn in Scene Tile style with the currently active scene marked ▶ and filled, existing scenes displaying their titles and timbre summaries, and empty slots shown dashed.

  • Slots 1–8. Displayed in a 4×2 grid. The currently sounding scene has full accent fill and on-accent text, plus the ▶ current tag. Highlighted/selected slot has a 3 px accent border and raised elevation. Empty slots have dashed border.
  • Touch targets. All action buttons (New scene, Rename, Duplicate, Move ◀ ▶, Clear) are at least 56 px tall.
  • Audio continuity. Sound continues uninterrupted while browsing and editing scenes. Edits are flagged with ◆ on the Live Set and become permanent when saved with WRITE (Scene).
  • Guards. The current scene cannot be cleared while it is the only remaining scene in the Live Set.
