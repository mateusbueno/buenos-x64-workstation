Velora 73: Part surface, FM and VA Sound sections

The Part surface spine (Timbre, Sound, Envelope & EQ, Zone & keyboard, Level & output, Effects, Arpeggiator) is unchanged. Sound keeps the list-left / fields-right layout; because FM and VA have dozens of parameters, the fields side is split into groups, chosen with a Segmented Control. Drawn at 1024 × 600 in the Stage and Studio themes. Only existing components and tokens are used: Top Bar, section rows (List Row tokens), Segmented Control, parameter fields (List Row tokens: surface fill, {rounded.md}, raised with a 3 px accent border when highlighted), Part Strip, Status Bar and Hint Bar. Every touch target is at least 56 px.

Note. The Part surface mockup for the other sections (Story 4.x) and part-surface-organ.html did not exist when this was drawn; this file draws the Part chrome (header, section list, bars) itself with the same tokens, and those mockups should match it.

Choosing a group: VALUE and touch

  • Touch. Tap a group in the strip: it opens at once. Tap a field to highlight it; tap it again to edit.
  • VALUE, one cursor. With the highlight on the group strip (3 px accent outline), turning VALUE steps through the groups and shows each one; WRITE opens the highlighted group and moves the highlight to its first field. In a group, turning VALUE moves the highlight from field to field (3 px accent border, raised fill); WRITE starts editing that field (◂ value ▸, label "Editing"); turning VALUE then changes the value, and WRITE or EXIT finishes. Edits sound immediately and follow the ◆ rule on the Part surface.
  • EXIT, one level back. Editing a value → field highlight → group strip → section list → Performance.
  • The 8 encoders keep their fixed functions on this part (DELAY, BRIGHTNESS, ATTACK, RELEASE, LOW, MID, MID FREQ, HIGH). Turning one never changes the group or the highlight.
  • Never colour only. Selected group = filled segment; highlighted field = border plus raised fill; editing = ◂ ▸ and the word "Editing"; warn = ! plus words.

Two oscillators with wave, octave, detune and pulse width; the mixer is four level rows.

The Filter with Cutoff being edited (◂ value ▸) above a filter response outline, then amp and filter envelopes side by side.

Voice holds voice mode, unison and glide.

Not-available rules

  • Wording. "Needs update 1.4" (n = the firmware that has the engine). Plain fact, no apology and no error code, with ! in warn colour. The engine type is never named (the Part Strip shows the category).
  • Sound. The part is silent and is never substituted by another timbre. The timbre name and category stay at full contrast; the settings stay in the Live Set and come back after the update.
  • Where it shows. Part Strip tag and Silent line; Status Bar warn takeover naming the count; Part surface Sound pane with an explanation, Change timbre and Install update. It opens no sheet by itself (never unprompted during performance).
