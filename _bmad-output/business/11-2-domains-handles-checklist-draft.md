## 4. What to verify at the registrar / platform (do this live, record date and screenshot)

Domains:
1. Availability right now, per TLD, via the registrar's own search (not a third-party cache).
2. Registrant is the company (legal name, CNPJ, address), not a personal identity; registrant e-mail on a company mailbox.
3. For .com.br: requirements for registrant documents and the CNPJ link: to confirm at Registro.br.
4. Registration term >= 2 years; auto-renew on; renewal price disclosed; payment method on file not tied to one person's card alone.
5. Registrar account: company e-mail, 2FA enabled (method recorded), recovery path recorded (backup codes stored where, second admin, recovery e-mail).
6. Registrar lock / transfer lock on; WHOIS privacy or Registro.br equivalent: to confirm what is offered.
7. DNS: parking/holding page only; no product claims, no third-party trademarks (NFR29, epics.md:282), no data collection until LGPD consent text and privacy notice exist (epics.md:6190-6192).
8. Mail: SPF/DKIM/DMARC planned before any e-mail from the domain (needed for Story 11.18 support and waitlist mail): to confirm with mail provider.
9. Conflicts: check if registered .com/.com.br/.net etc. already resolve to another party; note owner if so and escalate to the attorney from Story 11.1 (no action against third parties without legal advice).

Handles:
1. Availability right now on each platform (logged in as a fresh account); screenshot.
2. Create under a company e-mail, not a personal one; 2FA on; recovery path recorded.
3. Account type: business/creator where offered; platform terms and verification/age requirements: to confirm on each platform.
4. Profile = placeholder only: name, avatar placeholder (final avatar is a logo-designer deliverable, DESIGN.md "Deferred"), no product claims, no third-party trademarks.
5. Record URL, owner account, 2FA status (Story 11.2 AC2).
