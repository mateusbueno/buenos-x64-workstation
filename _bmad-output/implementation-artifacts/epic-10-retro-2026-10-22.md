## Findings

### Architecture & Persistence
- **Finding**: Centralized `SystemConfig` singleton manages device preferences with atomic persistence (`write_json_atomic` using temporary files + rename) and schema versioning (`schema_version = 1`).
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/system_config.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted. Prevents flash corruption across sudden power loss or reboots.
- **Finding**: Seven canonical settings sections implemented per UX specification (`Audio & Routing`, `Keyboard & Pedals`, `MIDI & Clock`, `Display & LED`, `System & Backup`, `About / Diagnostics`, `Legal & Licenses`).
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/settings_view.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.
- **Finding**: Dynamic conditional row rules enforced: `Aftertouch` row is conditionally hidden when `aftertouch_available == false` without leaving dead space.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/settings_view.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.

### UI / UX & Hardware Controls
- **Finding**: Theme selection cleanly toggles `Stage` (high contrast for live performance) and `Studio` (reduced glare). Drawbar styling contract strictly maintained (Stage: black heads 2px black edge; Studio: white heads 2px white edge).
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/settings_view.cpp`, `src/ui/performance_view.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.
- **Finding**: Brightness Quick Path (Story 10.5) requires holding `MCU_BTN_EXIT` while rotating the `VALUE` encoder.
  - *Discovery during dev*: `MCU_BTN_EXIT` previously handled actions on press, which fired immediate back navigation and prevented hold gestures.
  - *Resolution*: Moved back-navigation execution to button release when the encoder was not rotated. If rotated, brightness updates in 5% steps with a transient `NoticeToast` and back navigation is cancelled.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/navigation_controller.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Fixed now.
- **Finding**: `NoticeToast` interaction with quick path: Active toast previously intercepted `MCU_BTN_EXIT` press and consumed the event.
  - *Resolution*: Updated `NoticeToast` to coordinate with `exit_btn_down_` tracking so quick-path brightness adjustment functions consistently even if a toast is displayed.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/navigation_controller.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Fixed now.
- **Finding**: Large text (+25% typography, suppresses secondary labels/chips, truncates with ellipsis) and Reduced motion settings persist and reflect immediately across all surfaces.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/performance_view.cpp`, `src/ui/system_config.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.

### Localization & Quality Assurance
- **Finding**: Full localization catalogs provided for English (`en`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), and Spanish (`es`). Hot reloading of catalogs swaps labels without restarting the UI.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/locale/`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.
- **Finding**: Pseudo-localization engine (+40% expansion, accented characters, `[` `]` delimiters) integrated for QA to catch string clippings and text overflows.
  - *Reference*: `src/ui/locale.cpp`, `tests/ui/epic10_settings_test.cpp`
  - *Disposition*: Accepted.
