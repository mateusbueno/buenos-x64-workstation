# Epic 12 Retrospective: FM and VA Engines (Post-Launch)

## Epic summary

- **Epic goal (declared, `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md`, "Epic 12"):** A keyboardist with a shipped v1 unit receives a clean-room 6-operator FM engine (≥128 voices) and a clean-room virtual-analog engine (≥64 voices) by firmware update. Both run inside the shared voice pool behind the single engine interface (AD-7, AD-22), store their timbres in existing versioned formats without migration (FR14, AD-12), appear in the Library by category (never by engine type, FR58), and are edited in the Part surface and the Velora Studio Part Editor. The FM engine is parameter-compatible with classic 6-operator FM SysEx format, so players import their own SysEx banks and single voices from USB-A, Velora Studio, or over MIDI (FR121); no third-party patches ship. Development is Mac-first: engine DSP is built and tested in `engine/core` on the developer's Mac and in the offline renderer; voice budgets are accepted only on the target hardware. Marketing, UI, and panel names stay generic ("6-operator FM", "virtual analog"); no third-party trademarks anywhere (NFR29). GPL references (Dexed/MSFA) are used only as unshipped black-box test oracles (NFR28).

## Findings

### Aggregate views

1. **Clean-Room Architecture & DSP Parity (AD-7, AD-22, NFR28):**
   - The 6-operator FM synthesis engine was implemented from first principles (32 algorithms, sinusoidal phase accumulators, configurable feedback on operator 6, independent 4-stage operator rate/level envelopes, pitch envelope, multi-waveform LFO, velocity scaling).
   - Validated against an unshipped black-box reference oracle (Dexed/MSFA) with strict offline null testing, achieving sub-milliBel parity without copying code or infringing GPL.
   - The virtual-analog engine implemented dual band-limited oscillators (sawtooth, pulse/PWM, triangle, sine) with hard sync, dedicated sub-oscillator, noise generator, and zero-delay feedback (ZDF) 4-pole/2-pole multi-mode filter.
   - Both engines implement `velora::core::Engine` directly, running in the unified audio callback with zero heap allocations on the audio path (`VELORA_NONBLOCKING`).
   - Disposition: **accept**.

2. **Shared Voice Pool Arbiter & Floors (NFR8, Story 12.5, Story 12.7):**
   - Voice allocation for both engines integrates with `velora::core::VoiceArbiter` using calibrated cost units (4 units per voice for both FM and VA).
   - `VoiceArbiter` guarantees dedicated floors of at least 128 voices for FM and at least 64 voices for VA, while dynamically sharing the remaining headroom with sample and organ engines.
   - 64 headroom slots per engine ensure that 4 ms fade-out ramps (`kStealRampFrames = 192`) complete smoothly during voice stealing without voice starvation.
   - Disposition: **accept**.

3. **Format Forward/Backward Compatibility & Safe Rollback (FR14, FR45, AD-12, NFR12, Story 12.14):**
   - Live Sets created on earlier launch firmware (Release N) open on updated firmware (Release N+1) and play bit-for-bit identical (`max_diff == 0.0f` in automated render comparison).
   - When a unit rolls back from updated firmware to launch firmware, Live Sets utilizing FM and VA parts load cleanly without crashing:
     - Parts using FM or VA are marked with `core::PartState::kEngineNotAvailable` and remain strictly silent (`peak == 0.0f`).
     - Engine parameters are never mutated, wiped, or substituted with fallback sounds.
     - Protobuf preserves 100% of stored sound design parameters across the round-trip.
     - The remaining parts (Sample, Organ) continue to play without interruption.
   - Disposition: **accept**.

4. **Trademark Hygiene & Generic Naming Invariants (NFR29, NFR51, FR66):**
   - Strict adherence to generic naming across code, comments, Protobuf schemas, UI, Velora Studio, and documentation.
   - Engines are consistently identified as `"6-operator FM"` / `"FM de 6 operadores"` and `"virtual analog"` / `"analógico virtual"`.
   - Comprehensive automated scan confirmed zero forbidden third-party trademarks ("DX7", "Yamaha", "Roland", "Minimoog", "Moog", "Hammond", "Leslie", "Prophet", "Korg") across all three multilingual release note files (`v1.1.0-fm-va.en.md`, `v1.1.0-fm-va.pt-br.md`, `v1.1.0-fm-va.es.md`).
   - Disposition: **accept**.

5. **Decoupled Content Delivery & Built-in Init Timbres (FR46, FR56, FR58, Story 12.9):**
   - In compliance with FR46 and FR58, firmware updates do not bundle monolithic library content.
   - The 52-timbre curated factory FM/VA pack (`bundle.vlb`) is distributed and installed separately via the librarian service.
   - Built-in `"Init FM"` and `"Init VA"` timbres resolve immediately from `LibraryResolver` under category `Synth Poly` (14) directly from ROM/executable code, ensuring players always have immediate access to both engines without requiring external storage or content pack installation.
   - Disposition: **accept**.
