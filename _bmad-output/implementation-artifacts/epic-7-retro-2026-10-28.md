## Findings

### Aggregate views

1. **Zero-drift sample-accurate clock architecture.** The single tempo/transport clock on the RT plane derives step positions from integer frame counts and sample rate, completely eliminating cumulative float drift over extended runs (tested over 10 simulated minutes with 0-frame deviation).
   - Disposition: **accept**.
2. **Deterministic event-processor slot.** The serial phase of the audio block loop cleanly hosts the arpeggiator event processor ahead of voice allocation, with per-part RNG seeding ensuring 100% reproducible test output.
   - Disposition: **accept**.
3. **Seamless scene switching.** Arpeggiator settings swap cleanly as parameter snapshots across scenes without interrupting ongoing voices or releasing tails.
   - Disposition: **accept**.
4. **Physical external clock hardware validation.** The offline timing acceptance suite validates internal clock, synthetic external clock follower tracking, and clock-lost recovery. Physical DIN clock jitter testing on the target hardware remains open until board arrival.
   - Disposition: **defer** (action item 1).

### Diff-scope review lenses

- Review across stories verified RealtimeSanitizer safety (no allocations or locks in the block loop), exact UMP timestamp in-block offsets, range clamps on tempo (20.0 to 300.0 BPM), and graceful recovery from sudden MIDI clock interruption without hang or note-freeze.

### Behavior verification

- `ctest -R arp`: 38/38 tests passing (100% PASS), including `arpeggiator_tests`, `arp_timing_acceptance`, `arpeggiator_traffic_test`, `arp_render_a/b`, `arp_null_golden`, and all parameter error-rejection probes.
- Offline acceptance suite (`arp_timing_acceptance`): proves 0-frame deviation across all rates (1/4 to 1/32 including triplets) and swing factors (50% to 75%).
- UI integration: LVGL simulator shows the Part surface Arpeggiator section, Top Bar tempo/clock display, and `CLOCK LOST` warning takeover.
