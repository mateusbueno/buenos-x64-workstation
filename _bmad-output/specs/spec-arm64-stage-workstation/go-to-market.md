# Go-to-Market

## Naming

- **Brand:** Ladiani (Ladislau + Oliani).
- **Model:** Velora. v1 = **Ladiani Velora 73**, Pro = **Ladiani Velora 73 Pro**, desktop app = **Velora Studio**.
- **Preliminary screening (Oct 2026):**
  - WIPO Global Brand Database shows no conflicting marks in Nice classes 15 and 9.
  - The domains ladiani.com, .com.br, .net, .io, .music and .audio were free.
  - `@ladiani` is taken on Instagram and YouTube. `@ladianimusic` and `@ladiani.instruments` were free on both.
- **Before branding spend:**
  - A formal clearance search by a trademark attorney.
  - INPI filing in classes 15 and 9, then a Madrid Protocol filing for export.
  - Registering the domains and handles.

## Market and price

- Launch market: Brazil first, export later.
- Local street prices (Oct 2026): Nord Electro 7 73 at R$ 22,990, Nord Electro 6D 73 at R$ 18,990, Yamaha YC73 at R$ 17,590.
- **v1:** R$ 17,990–19,990. It competes on value at the Electro 6D/YC73 price, not by undercutting: a library of hundreds of GB, organ, USB audio interface, made in Brazil.
- **Pro:** R$ 24,990+, adding a sequencer, 16 parts, and 32 GB.
- Rough low-volume landed cost is about R$ 11,000 (unverified). Sales taxes (~25–35%) and a dealer margin (~30%) rule out a retail price below R$ 15,000.

## Channel

Hybrid: the first ~100 units sell direct to musicians, which keeps the dealer margin. Retail partners come in after scale.

## Certification

- No radio in v1, so no Anatel homologation is needed.
- An off-the-shelf power supply that complies with Inmetro (the exact requirement is still to be confirmed).
- Safety and EMC tested to IEC 62368-1 anyway, so CE and FCC are easier for later export.
