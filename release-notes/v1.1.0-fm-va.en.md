# Velora 73 Firmware Update v1.1.0

## Release Notes (English)

Velora 73 Firmware v1.1.0 delivers two brand-new, clean-room synthesis engines to all existing Velora 73 instruments, expanding sound design capabilities while preserving every Live Set, user sample library, scene, and custom setting.

### New Sound Engines

- **6-Operator FM Synthesis Engine:**
  - Full clean-room implementation of 6-operator frequency modulation synthesis.
  - 32 classic algorithms with sinusoidal oscillators, flexible operator routing, and configurable feedback.
  - Dedicated multi-stage envelope generators per operator with rate and level modulation, plus independent pitch envelope.
  - Comprehensive LFO with multiple waveforms (sine, triangle, saw up, saw down, square, sample & hold) and key sync.
  - Dynamic voice allocation with a guaranteed floor of at least 128 voices in the shared voice pool.
  - Full SysEx voice and bank compatibility (32-voice bulk dumps and single voice files) via USB-A, Velora Studio, or standard MIDI.

- **Virtual Analog (VA) Subtractive Synthesis Engine:**
  - Full clean-room virtual analog synthesis engine.
  - Dual oscillators supporting continuously variable waveforms (sawtooth, pulse/square with PWM, triangle, sine) and oscillator hard sync.
  - Dedicated sub-oscillator and pink/white noise source.
  - Zero-delay feedback (ZDF) topology-preserving transform filter with selectable 4-pole (24 dB/oct) and 2-pole (12 dB/oct) low-pass, band-pass, and high-pass modes.
  - Dual dedicated ADSR envelope generators for filter cutoff and amplifier amplitude.
  - Multi-waveform modulation LFO with key sync and routing matrix.
  - Guaranteed voice floor of at least 64 voices in the shared voice pool.

### Library & Factory Content

- **Built-in Init Timbres:**
  - "Init FM" and "Init VA" timbres are built directly into the system firmware under the `Synth Poly` category.
  - Instantly accessible from the front panel or Velora Studio without requiring external storage or content pack installation.
- **Factory FM/VA Sound Pack (Optional Separate Download):**
  - Curated library of 52 handcrafted timbres across electric pianos, mallet instruments, synth basses, brass, leads, strings, and pads.
  - Distributed as a standard `.vlb` content pack installable via Velora Studio or USB-A.

### System & Performance Invariants

- **Full Backward Compatibility:**
  - Every Live Set, scene, setlist, and imported sample library created on launch firmware opens and renders identically with zero parameter deviation.
  - Robust A/B system rollback: if a unit rolls back to previous firmware, Live Sets utilizing the new engines load safely with those parts placed in an unavailable warning state while keeping all stored sound parameters 100% intact.
- **Stage Latency & Stability:**
  - Strict key-to-audio latency budget maintained under 5 ms across 8 simultaneous parts with full effect chains.
  - Zero audio dropouts (xruns) under sustained multi-engine performance.
- **Atomic A/B Update:**
  - Fully verified and signed dual-slot (A/B) installation protecting against unexpected power loss during updates.
